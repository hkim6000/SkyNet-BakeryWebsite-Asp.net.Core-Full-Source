Our story

Bread, given the time it needs

A small bakery with a long fermentation

Hearthstone started with one oven and one jar of starter. Today we still mix every dough by hand, let it rest overnight and bake in small batches so the crust is crisp when it reaches you.

We keep the menu short on purpose. A handful of loaves, a few pastries, cakes made to order and cookies for the road. Everything is baked for the orders we have, which means less waste and fresher bread.

That is why we ask you to order ahead. Pick a day and a time window, and we will have it ready, warm, at the counter or at your door.

How we bake

  • Slow dough

    Most loaves rest for at least sixteen hours. The wait gives flavour and a crumb that keeps.

  • Short ingredient lists

    Flour, water, salt and time for the bread. Butter, not shortcuts, for the pastry.

  • Baked to order

    We bake for the day’s orders and a few extra for the counter. Sold out means sold out.
